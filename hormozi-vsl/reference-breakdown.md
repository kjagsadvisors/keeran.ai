Traffic asks for a quote. The lead is contacted in under 60 seconds. The VSL is sent to them **between the quote request and the in-home appointment**, so they show up to the visit already sold on the process.

Item (3) reads "Frame all sales as package then DS…". The last word is cut off by the chart edge. In context it is most likely "then downsell", but that is a reading, not a quote.

## What the lesson says, in short

1. A VSL is four blocks: **intro → unique thing → objections → CTA**.
2. The intro is five beats, **Promise, Pain, Proof, Plan, Pic**, in at most about 90 seconds.
3. The unique thing is your named process, in 1–3 minutes: "here's how our thing works."
4. Each objection gets the same four moves: **what they think, why that's wrong, what's actually right, proof**. Do this for every major objection (he writes "1–5").
5. End on the one thing you want them to do.
6. Make two versions with the same script and different examples.
7. The VSL's job in this funnel is to **pre-frame** the appointment. Only the CTA changes between uses.

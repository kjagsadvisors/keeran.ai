# Reference: Hormozi's two lead magnet lessons, step by step

Two of his own videos teach the whole method from start to finish. Everything below is paraphrased; links go to the second.

- **L1:** "Watch This To Generate 1000s of Leads (In Any Niche)", 31 min, 872k views ([youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0))
- **L2:** "Giving Away Free Stuff Will Make You Rich", 12 min, a chapter preview of *$100M Leads* ending in a 7-step checklist ([youtube.com/watch?v=7NMH1oAkgLY](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7NMH1oAkgLY))

## L1: what, why, the three types, four ways to deliver, the name, the CTA

**1. The problem it fixes** ([0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=0s))
- Most businesses send traffic straight to "buy" or "get a quote". Most visitors aren't ready, so they leave and never come back.
- "Book a call" only works on an audience that already knows you. Its real job is to capture contact details, so ask what would capture *more* of them, from people who'd plausibly buy. ([1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=60s))

**2. The definition** ([2:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=121s))
- A lead magnet is a mini offer: a complete solution to a narrow problem, free or cheap, that shows who's interested.
- Solving it reveals the next problem, and the core offer solves that one.
- People who take something free or low-cost now are more likely to buy the related, pricier thing later. (He reads this from page 31 of *$100M Leads*.)

**3. Why it works**
- Same 100 people. Ask them to buy and maybe one says yes. Help them first and then ask, and you get three to five times as many. You convert more of the same traffic without spending more on ads. ([3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=181s))
- His own breakthrough came in 2016. His webinar didn't work, so he swapped it for a screen-recorded "free case study" of how a small San Diego gym added 213 members and $112k. The next morning the calendar was full. ([3:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=211s))
- A webinar can be a lead magnet. Sophisticated audiences read it as a pitch, though. "Here's what I did, watch it when you like" got far more opt-ins. ([4:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=271s))
- **Test the packaging more than the contents.** The headline alone can change demand 2x, 3x or 10x, as long as the contents clearly solve the problem. ([5:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=301s))
- People who pay with time now are more likely to pay with money later. Lead magnets work; bad lead magnets don't. ([7:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=424s))

**4. The objections**
- *Freebie seekers:* only give it to qualified people. Add a qualifying drop-down and send unqualified people somewhere else. ([8:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=517s))
- *Giving away too much:* don't solve the problem your core product solves. Solve one that leads to it, so that solving it *creates* the hunger for what you sell. Sell at the point of greatest deprivation: the dessert after the entree, food after the water. ([9:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=549s))

**5. The three types** ([10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=611s))

| Type | What it does | His examples | Best when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Reveal a problem** (his favourite) | A diagnosis that shows a problem they didn't know about, or how bad a known one is. Bonus: show what solved looks like and the gap. | Free website speed test: each second of load time costs about 3% of conversions, so 9 s → 3 s is about 18% more business. His top Allen ad was "4 reasons why you'll never have a million-dollar agency". | Problems that get worse while they wait: posture, termites, back taxes, cash flow ([11:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=672s)) |
| **Sample / trial** | Give the solution, then take it away, so its absence creates the want. Limit uses, quantity, time, or a mix. | The Costco sampler. Gym Launch gave four months of free agency leads with the system, then they paid to keep the leads coming. ([13:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=793s)) | Prospects who already know the problem |
| **One step of many** | Do one step of a multi-step process completely; the next steps are the sale. | First coat of paint; laser hair removal (6–8 sessions); the first two videos of a course; a stylist giving you your colours first ([6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=363s)) | Complex products and services ([15:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=917s)) |

- **Be specific about the negatives and modest about the positives.** If you name someone's problems exactly, they'll assume you can fix them. That also keeps claims compliant and expectations realistic. ([12:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=733s))

**6. Four ways to deliver** ([15:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=947s))

All four can be combined. His scaling road map is a tool plus information.
1. **Software / tools:** calculating spreadsheets, assessment tools, templates, small apps. Example: a URL-in, site-audit-out tool. ([16:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=977s))
2. **Information:** mini courses, guides, expert interviews, static templates. It costs nothing to deliver, scales infinitely and has no operational drag. ([16:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=1007s))
3. **Services:** free audits with some implementation, same-day work, anything done for you. "People sleep on this." Only give it to qualified prospects. ([18:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=1098s))
   - **His math:** one hour of labour costs you $25 and is worth $250 or more to them. If one in four buys, a customer costs you $100. ([19:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=1158s))
   - **His first business:** he trained people free for a year to collect testimonials. When demand outgrew him, almost all of them agreed to start paying. ([20:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=1219s))
4. **Physical:** his books are lead magnets. So is a "CEO" shirt you only get by proving you're a CEO, which builds a qualified list. ([20:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=1250s), [21:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=1312s))

**7. Name it** ([22:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=1342s))
- The name sets the engagement rate more than anything else. **The real secret: ask your audience.**
- Advertise the result, not the vehicle. His "big booty boot camp" sold the same six weeks of squats and deadlifts that "deadlift and squat seminar" wouldn't. ([22:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=1373s))
- **How to test** ([23:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=1434s)):
  - With money: small ad tests on the headlines, for a defined avatar.
  - Without money: poll your stories ("if you're not a business owner, don't answer"), text people "which one?", or post "comment A or B".
  - He tested six headlines for *$100M Leads*, plus the image and the subhead. Two years writing the book justified two days testing the name.
- **Four naming formulas** ([25:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=1527s)):
  1. **Number + outcome + time frame:** "3 emails that turn cold leads into clients in 24 hours"
  2. **How to [good thing] without [bad thing], even if [insecurity]**
  3. **The [adjective] [thing] that [good thing]:** "the lazy funnel template that converts like crazy"
  4. **X mistakes that [cost you the goal]:** "4 mistakes keeping your business under $1M a year"

**8. Make money with it: the CTA** ([26:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=1595s))
- **Ask:** most people build a great lead magnet and forget to ask. Salespeople who ask most often close the most.
- **Value between asks:** ask as often as you can, and give value between asks so they keep listening. ([27:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=1625s))
- **The formula:** clear, not clever; say exactly what to do; give a reason to do it now. Scarcity or urgency if you have it, but any reason beats none (the photocopier line study). ([28:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=1688s), [29:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=1750s))
  - The audio skips point 2 of his three; it's only on screen.
- **Real scarcity:** service businesses (he says 78% of businesses) have a real capacity limit, so state it, e.g. "4 more customers fills us". Bigger businesses can run rolling cohorts ("we start this many a week"). ([30:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=1811s))
- **Where CTAs go:** inside the lead magnet, and before and after it. ([31:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=1872s))

## L2: the 7-step checklist he uses for any business

**Before the steps:**
- People buy on a prediction of future value. The easiest way to earn that prediction is to give value before they buy. ([0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7NMH1oAkgLY&t=0s))
- **The core offer is whatever makes you the most money.** If the lead magnet starts out-earning it, that's your core offer now. ([1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7NMH1oAkgLY&t=60s))
- **Not a partial solution.** Six minutes of a twelve-minute video with "click for the rest" loses trust. Give complete work on a narrow problem, which then opens the next one. ([1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7NMH1oAkgLY&t=60s), [2:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7NMH1oAkgLY&t=121s))
- **Massage math:** $100 massages get 1 yes in 100. $10 trials get 20, and a third of those buy packages: 6 customers. What the lead magnet costs to deliver is what you pay to get a customer, and it should still lower that cost overall. ([3:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7NMH1oAkgLY&t=184s))
- **Map the journey:** before, during and after. For weight loss: a pre-loaded grocery list by body type, then "clean and prep it in under 60 minutes", then "store and portion it". Solve the first one or two completely and charge for the third. ([5:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7NMH1oAkgLY&t=305s))
- **Things that cost you to deliver are valued more.** A free PDF gets little credit unless it's exceptional. Free real work gets taken up far more, and the math still works. "Your free stuff should be better than their paid stuff." ([6:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7NMH1oAkgLY&t=395s))
  - This sits in tension with L1, which praises information for costing nothing. Resolution: info is fine, but it has to be exceptional.

**The seven steps** ([7:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7NMH1oAkgLY&t=455s))
1. **The problem, and who it's for.** Knowing who changes how you name it, package it and deliver it.
2. **How you'll solve it:**
   - reveal a problem (an assessment);
   - a trial (for people who already know they have the problem);
   - one step of many;
   - or a combination.
3. **How you'll deliver it:** information, service, physical or software. Pick whatever the prospect values most and is most likely to lead to a sale. ([8:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7NMH1oAkgLY&t=487s))
4. **Package it:** name, subhead and image, all tested. He calls this the most important step. ([8:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7NMH1oAkgLY&t=518s))
   - His book title tests: "advertising" beat "promotions and advertising" 69–31, then "leads" beat "advertising" 58–42, then "leads" beat "marketing".
5. **Make it fast and easy to consume.** Offer several formats. *$100M Offers* sold in almost equal quarters across audio, print, ebook and video. ([9:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7NMH1oAkgLY&t=579s))
6. **Make it good.** Fluff loses that buyer, and they then tell other people not to buy. If people don't tell others about your lead magnet, it failed. If you've never put more than 10% into one, put in 100%. ([10:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7NMH1oAkgLY&t=610s))
7. **Make the next step easy.** CTAs clear, not clever, with several ways to act: phone, URL, QR. Matching how people prefer to act can double or triple response. ([11:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7NMH1oAkgLY&t=672s))

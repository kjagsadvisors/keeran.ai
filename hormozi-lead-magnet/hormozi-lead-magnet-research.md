## 1. What it's for

- **The definition:** a complete solution to one narrow problem, given away before the paid offer, so that solving it
  exposes the next problem, which the paid offer solves. Don't try to solve their whole problem for free.
  ([Leads launch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k834E6OTGTM&t=2251s), [Secret Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SmiOK8Yun4s&t=155s), [Email Campaign](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OpeN4O5myIg&t=283s))
- **Most buyers want "a little bit first".** He puts it at 80% or more. "Get more info" is too big a jump from zero, so
  it mostly attracts desperate buyers. ([Secret Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SmiOK8Yun4s&t=155s))
- **High-information vs low-information buyers.** A minority buy straight from an ad. The other ~97% need more time and
  information, and giving it to everyone closes more people overall. ([Easy Mode 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ds_Qp2U5I8U&t=715s))
- **Solve one loop and they assume the rest.** The lead magnet is one small problem-solution loop inside their bigger
  goal; solve it accurately and they assume you can solve the others. With a reveal-a-problem magnet, "this is exactly
  what I'm struggling with" raises their belief that you can help. ([Easy Mode 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ds_Qp2U5I8U&t=775s), [Easy Mode 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ds_Qp2U5I8U&t=994s))
- **His claim for the upside:** done right, a lead magnet can 2x, 5x, sometimes 10x leads. As a back-of-envelope:
  1 buyer in 100 from a direct pitch becomes about 3 in 100 with a free step first. Both are illustrations, not
  measurements. ([Leads launch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k834E6OTGTM&t=2281s), [Secret Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SmiOK8Yun4s&t=185s))
- **It's a filter as well as a magnet.** Pull in the right people and weed out the wrong ones. ([Stranger $1M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nrounb8NlFQ&t=916s))
- **Reputation:** 99% will never buy, but they judge you on your free stuff and tell others.
  ([Leads launch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k834E6OTGTM&t=2313s), [Secret Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SmiOK8Yun4s&t=308s))
- **Reciprocity needs a visible hard cost.** Free only creates the pull to repay if they can see what it cost you.
  ([3 Fastest Ways](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ovL6Z5z0jxQ&t=834s))
- **Content is the cheapest lead magnet:** hundreds of tiny ones with no opt-in, a "pre-lead magnet" to the lead magnet.
  ([Secret Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SmiOK8Yun4s&t=185s), [Get Rich Young](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DfXy_PzlC0o&t=1184s))
- **A paid front end counts too.** He calls a lash studio's $99 promo a lead magnet: it isn't built to profit. Watch
  how many step-one buyers take step two. ([Stranger $2.3M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-Koq14DPXC4&t=710s))
- **The usual chain:** ad → lead magnet → VSL → qualify → call. Non-buyers go into email or a group and get "reheated"
  later. ([Easy Mode 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ds_Qp2U5I8U&t=1670s))
- **Where scaling stalls:** when ad spend rises and ROAS falls, the first thing that limits you is the lead magnet.
  ([Top Questions](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QwEb78e5a8Y&t=725s))
- **It isn't always the lever.**
  - Direct "buy my X" ads can get higher immediate ROAS, but they cap out at the warm few percent.
  - For a janitorial company or a craft box he said the offer or channel matters more.
  - For roofers he called the lead magnet mostly a fresh hook.

  ([34.2M Views](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eW04CvHKvMo&t=4254s), [Hotline](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-HbwUdqUcEs&t=7985s), [34.2M Views](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eW04CvHKvMo&t=9473s))

## 2. Types and delivery

- **Three types:** reveal a problem, a sample or free trial, one step of a multi-step process. Type 2 is called "free
  trial" in some videos and "sample of the solution" in others.
  ([Stranger $3M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n6SHLmKcY0E&t=921s), [Easy Mode 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ds_Qp2U5I8U&t=838s), [3 Fastest Ways](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ovL6Z5z0jxQ&t=896s))
- **Reveal-a-problem examples he uses:**
  - his $100M Scaling Roadmap, which shows which of 10 stages you're at and what's holding you back;
  - "Gym Launch Secrets", which shows everything wrong with your gym until it feels overwhelming and you want help;
  - a DM framework that shows why you aren't getting replies.

  ([Easy Mode 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ds_Qp2U5I8U&t=963s), [3 Fastest Ways](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ovL6Z5z0jxQ&t=926s))
- **Challenges:** a 7- or 14-day challenge pulled from what already works for customers. It hits all four parts of the
  value equation: a clear outcome, believable, fast because it's time-boxed, and easy. It works as a mini version of a
  slow, big outcome. ([Email Campaign](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OpeN4O5myIg&t=314s), [Email Campaign](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OpeN4O5myIg&t=405s))
- **Services:** "a lead magnet does not have to be an ebook" or automated.
  - Examples: a personal style assessment, a FaceTime kitchen or grocery walk-through, five free one-to-one calls, a
    personalised Loom showing opportunities, a live walk-through of a gym's sales setup.
  - In fitness he thinks time and effort beat free materials.

  ([Secret Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SmiOK8Yun4s&t=338s), [1/0 Live](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9OsUfwTTslc&t=4995s), [Answers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IG9IWQVmxqk&t=1987s), [Scaled Sales Team](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=okA9Yt2KZuk&t=899s))
- **Tools:** an internal spreadsheet that shows a bar its revenue opportunity from its top sellers, and his roadmap
  diagnostic. ([Stranger $3M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n6SHLmKcY0E&t=981s))
- **Other forms:** free communities, events, books (his own are lead magnets), the first module of a course, a "secret
  chapter", and free facility access sold up to personal training.
  ([Paid Ads 30 Min](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fSbqaTlWaYI&t=1754s), [Runs Without You](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TLpLmhWqKEU&t=3517s), [13 Ways](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lc8DNduiwKA&t=2343s))
- **Costly or personal magnets are fine for qualified people only.** Say so on the page.
  ([Stranger $1M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nrounb8NlFQ&t=1415s))

## 3. Making one

- **The 7 steps:**
  1. pick the best problem;
  2. how to solve it;
  3. how to deliver it;
  4. test the name;
  5. make it easy to consume;
  6. make it really good;
  7. make it easy for them to say they want more.

  He says #7 is the one people miss. His page-45 recap lists only the first six.
  ([Leads launch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k834E6OTGTM&t=2404s), [Secret Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SmiOK8Yun4s&t=277s))
- **One avatar first.** A "get your first five clients" magnet draws beginners who aren't your buyer today. Look at the
  top 20% of customers by spend, or the ones who've stayed two years, and build for what they have in common.
  ([Easy Mode 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ds_Qp2U5I8U&t=899s), [Get Rich Young](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DfXy_PzlC0o&t=1153s))
- **Make sure it attracts the buyer you want.** A client's book about quitting his job pulled in employees. Hormozi told
  him to write it about the business instead. ([Being Fast](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fcyIoN8CUOk&t=85s))
- **Pick the problem.** Choose the tiniest one you can solve completely and so well it blows them away. Picking the
  wrong one is among the biggest mistakes, because some problems lead to purchases and some just waste your time.
  ([Easy Mode 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ds_Qp2U5I8U&t=684s), [Leads launch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k834E6OTGTM&t=2374s))
- **Where to find problems:** the comments on your content, and the friction customers hit when they implement your
  product. ([13 Years Marketing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=reisEL_D7xc&t=3914s))
- **Build it from what already works.** Pull something out of your product, or peel off the first step of your service
  that you normally charge for and template it. SOPs and checklists you already use count.
  ([Email Campaign](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OpeN4O5myIg&t=283s), [Stranger $1M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nrounb8NlFQ&t=1415s), [No BS Advice](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cnbHgYYHzyE&t=5773s))
- **Narrow:** "deep is where the dollars are". A specific magnet beats a generic one.
  ([13 Years Marketing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=reisEL_D7xc&t=4035s), [Launching Live](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B2QbLzHvKbg&t=21001s))
  - **Tension:** elsewhere he prefers the widest net first to get flow, adding quality filters only once volume is huge.
    ([Lead Quality](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Kk6Vdfdwmxw&t=290s))
- **Good enough to charge for.** Would you charge $100, $1,000 or $10,000 for it? "Give away the secrets, sell the
  implementation." High-ticket done-for-you providers should put every secret in.
  ([13 Years Marketing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=reisEL_D7xc&t=3974s), [34.2M Views](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eW04CvHKvMo&t=4284s))
- **Easy to consume:** risk-free, easy and fast, and they shouldn't have to work for the value. You pre-digest it.
  **Speed beats size:** a faster, less valuable magnet beats a slower, more valuable one.
  ([Stranger $1M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nrounb8NlFQ&t=1234s), [Stranger $1M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nrounb8NlFQ&t=1324s))
- **Make it work with what they already have,** to defuse the main objection (a stylist's version: "using your existing
  closet"). ([Secret Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SmiOK8Yun4s&t=746s))
- **Make it feel personal cheaply.** Ask a few questions (skin tone, hair, eyes), then route the answers. People feel it
  was made for them. ([Stranger $1M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nrounb8NlFQ&t=1748s))
- **No heavy first step.** A photo upload at opt-in was flagged: people wander off. Move it to the follow-up.
  ([Stranger $1M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nrounb8NlFQ&t=1918s))
- **Ship it.** Don't polish so long it never goes out. His VSLs are mostly still version one, plus new objection answers.
  ([Secret Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SmiOK8Yun4s&t=532s))

## 4. The name and the page

- **Name first.** Capture is the first job, and before they consume it the title is all they see. Test 10, 20, even 30
  names. ([1.5M Q&A](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lidFtb1Ga0o&t=1675s))
- **Headlines take about 80% of the attention** and frame how the rest is read. Test them first.
  ([Secret Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SmiOK8Yun4s&t=715s))
- **Name the freebie, not the sale:** "let's get coffee", not "marry me". His recipe: the result ("yay") plus the
  biggest insecurity or objection ("boo"), e.g. "free lookbook to dress for your tone using your existing closet".
  ([Secret Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SmiOK8Yun4s&t=746s))
- **His favourite lead magnet:** "how to know if a girl wants you to kiss her". It answers one question, carries high
  pain and high promise. ([Easy Mode 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ds_Qp2U5I8U&t=653s))
- **Names he proposed for real businesses:**
  - "100 days of content in 100 minutes";
  - "seven revenue opportunities you can set up in 30 days, free" (he said not to call it an
    "audit", which sounds boring);
  - "top 10 drinks you shouldn't be serving";
  - "six ways to decrease CPAs".

  ([AMA](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_ooj0V4hpgw&t=1848s), [34.2M Views](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eW04CvHKvMo&t=5415s), [Stranger $3M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n6SHLmKcY0E&t=861s))
- **Narrow the name to the buyer.** "Fill all 10 chairs in 30 days" pulls salon owners, a "free report on the hair
  industry" pulls anyone, and "first five cut-and-colour clients" pulls stylists.
  ([Insane Money](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QTZsh3BgOwY&t=133s))
- **Say it's free.** A clinic ad he reviewed never did. ([Stranger $5M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sBJppqCeFGI&t=920s))
- **The opt-in page:**
  - a headline about what they get;
  - a sub-headline that clarifies or qualifies ("without X, Y, Z");
  - an image of the thing (make it look like a book) or a before/after;
  - the opt-in;
  - below the fold, only what helps them move forward.

  For cold traffic, remove the logo and business name from the top.
  ([Stranger $3M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n6SHLmKcY0E&t=2445s), [Stranger $3M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n6SHLmKcY0E&t=2620s), [Stranger $1M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nrounb8NlFQ&t=1475s))
- **Congruence:** if you're high-end, the ad, magnet, qualification, price and product must all look it. The page must
  keep the ad's promise. ([Secret Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SmiOK8Yun4s&t=1393s), [Easy Mode 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ds_Qp2U5I8U&t=562s))
- **New wrapper, same thing.** Re-skin by season or pain about once a quarter ("Lean by Halloween", "Slim for Santa",
  "42-day fix"). You can swap the title on a live magnet and keep sending the old one to past opt-ins.
  ([Email Campaign](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OpeN4O5myIg&t=634s), [Start From Nothing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=unshZobTt6Q&t=668s), [1.5M Q&A](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lidFtb1Ga0o&t=1736s))

## 5. After the opt-in

- **Qualify on the same form,** with real BANT questions (budget, authority, need, timing) plus income. "Are you
  willing to invest in your future?" is a fake qualifier. Qualified people go to the scheduler; unqualified people get a
  free resource. Adding friction here has almost always made him more money, even with fewer calls.
  ([Runs Without You](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TLpLmhWqKEU&t=3551s), [Stranger $1M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nrounb8NlFQ&t=1718s), [Launching Live](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B2QbLzHvKbg&t=21281s))
- **Friction rule:** consuming should be effortless, but qualifying and booking should have friction.
  ([Stranger $1M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nrounb8NlFQ&t=1234s), [Launching Live](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B2QbLzHvKbg&t=21281s))
- **Thank-you page:** offer a personalised version ("book a call, we'll tailor it to your bar").
  ([Stranger $3M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n6SHLmKcY0E&t=861s))
- **Run the call off the lead magnet:** go through what they got, take a few of their numbers, then explain how you'd
  help if it's a fit. ([Stranger $3M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n6SHLmKcY0E&t=3258s))
- **The flow:** opt-in → VSL above the scheduler → after booking, the VSL again plus "how to prepare for your call".
  - A caller converting about 1% of opt-ins had skipped the VSL [C].
  - Host the VSL off YouTube.

  ([Stranger $3M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n6SHLmKcY0E&t=1337s), [Launching Live](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B2QbLzHvKbg&t=21218s))
- **"I'll go through your free stuff first":** agree. If it works, they'll hit the next problem you solve; if it
  doesn't, you solve it. ([Sales Training 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=StVqS0jD7Ls&t=7424s))
- **Delivery email (his own):**
  1. Tease what's coming so they stay subscribed.
  2. Deliver the promise.
  3. Ask them to reply "yes" for a bonus, which keeps you out of the promotions tab.
  4. Sell staying on the list before giving the reward.

  The bad cases are a bad magnet, or a good magnet with no reason to stay.
  ([Email Marketing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pLhQOYMGa88&t=1735s), [Email Marketing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pLhQOYMGa88&t=1857s))
- **Deliver it two ways:** by email, and as a link right on the page. ([Stranger $3M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n6SHLmKcY0E&t=1368s))

## 6. Promoting it

- **Ads:** call out the avatar ("attention salon owners"), lead with a pain-based hook, then offer the free thing.
  Run two ads that differ only in the magnet offered. ([Insane Money](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QTZsh3BgOwY&t=163s), [Stranger $3M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n6SHLmKcY0E&t=861s))
- **Content to ad:** post lots of niche content, add a CTA to the winners, and run those as ads with the magnet as the
  offer. ([Hotline](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-HbwUdqUcEs&t=5183s))
- **Comment-a-word, then a DM:**
  - The ManyChat flow he recommended: the CTA in the first line of the video, an automatic DM to new followers, then a
    sorting question ("here for free stuff, or to grow your business?").
  - Keep the CTA to about 3 seconds; 6 was too long and hurt view-through.

  ([AMA](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_ooj0V4hpgw&t=1818s), [34.2M Views](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eW04CvHKvMo&t=2670s))
- **Lighter asks reach further.**
  - "Comment for a free assessment, then book a call in DMs" is the heaviest ask you can make; he'd use a cheat sheet
    plus a triage question instead.
  - His roadmap ads have lower immediate ROAS than ads straight to a workshop, but higher ROI over 60–90 days and about
    10x the reach.

  ([34.2M Views](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eW04CvHKvMo&t=2766s))
- **Rotate magnets.** People who already have one won't respond to it again. If posting daily, about 2 posts a week
  carry a book-a-call CTA and the rest carry lead magnets. ([34.2M Views](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eW04CvHKvMo&t=2702s), [34.2M Views](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eW04CvHKvMo&t=2828s))
- **CTAs at the start, middle and end of videos,** with about 5 short variants of each to beat banner blindness.
  ([1.2M Followers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WrCt0R3FBFs&t=253s))
- **Partners:** let partners bundle your magnet free or sell it to their audience and keep the money.
  ([Investor Q&A](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=--9kOXNCUdE&t=1206s))
- **Outbound:** if nobody replies, give away more in the opening message.
  ([1/0 Live](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9OsUfwTTslc&t=4904s))
- **Inside a VSL:** tease the tip early, deliver it about a third of the way in, then mention the 28 others.
  ([Easy Mode 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ds_Qp2U5I8U&t=1446s))

## 7. Numbers

- **Opt-in benchmarks:**
  - 20–50% (sometimes 60%) for a page with qualified traffic and a clearly defined magnet.
  - 30% for high-intent search traffic.
  - A bar consultancy's page at 7% [C] he read as about a 4x opportunity.
  - A caller's 5% opt-in [C] he called "the lever by a mile".

  ([Stranger $1M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nrounb8NlFQ&t=1384s), [Stranger $3M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n6SHLmKcY0E&t=3288s), [1.5M Q&A](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lidFtb1Ga0o&t=2112s))
- **Tailored beats generic [C]:** a general course converted about 0.5% of viewers; a PDF tailored to each YouTube
  video converted 10–15%. ([Launching Live](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B2QbLzHvKbg&t=20971s))
- **Email challenge:** about 10,000 registered, 800 bought at $2,000, for $1.6M, from a somewhat warm list.
  ([Email Campaign](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OpeN4O5myIg&t=253s))
- **$97 vs $47 front end:** about the same number of buyers at twice the money, and the $97 buyers spent more later.
  ([Stranger $3M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n6SHLmKcY0E&t=1012s))
- **Launch pitch claims (promotional):**
  - 10 businesses averaging about $35k extra a month by month 3;
  - a 17x lift in applications at acquisition.com in 2022.

  ([Leads launch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k834E6OTGTM&t=2344s), [Leads launch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k834E6OTGTM&t=2435s))
- **Group funnels:** good operators move about 10% of group opt-ins to the next level, and calls close at 30–50%.
  ([Paid Ads 30 Min](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fSbqaTlWaYI&t=1785s))
- **What a customer costs:** the magnet's delivery cost × the number of magnets per customer.
  - $25 of labour given to four people for one sale = $100.
  - A $10 trial massage: 20 yeses from 100 instead of 1, and 6 buy.

  ([reference L1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0&t=1188s), [reference L2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7NMH1oAkgLY&t=184s))

## 8. Mistakes

- **No lead magnet, or a bad one.** Most are bad, and your first one probably will be.
  ([Easy Mode 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ds_Qp2U5I8U&t=622s))
- **The wrong problem,** **the wrong avatar,** or **a vague magnet** when a specific one is possible.
- **Hard or slow to consume,** or a heavy first step.
- **Giving a costly magnet to everyone,** or using a fake qualifier.
- **Fear of friction** at the qualifying step.
- **The same magnet CTA on every post,** long CTAs, or a heavy ask.
- **Sending the magnet with nothing else,** so they have no reason to stay on the list.
- **Stacking two magnets in one funnel.** A/B them instead.
- **Optimising only for opt-ins.** Track through to sales and LTV.
- **Mixing cold-traffic and high-intent numbers.**
- **Polishing forever instead of shipping.**
- **Logo and business name on top of a cold-traffic page,** or an ad that never says "free".

## 9. Testing

- **Test the name first and in volume:** 10–30 titles as simple ads, compared on CTR.
- **Without budget:** story polls, texts, "comment A or B" (see the reference breakdown).
- **When the opt-in is the weakest step,** put all the testing there: clarity, simplicity, more visuals, more trust.
- **Test promos on a slice first:** 10% of the list, one to three times, then send to everyone.
- **One lever at a time:** lead magnet, creative, CRO, sales efficiency.
- **Measure:** UTMs on every link to the magnet, and return per $1 at set days after opt-in.
- **Re-test after every seasonal re-wrap.**

([1.5M Q&A](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lidFtb1Ga0o&t=1706s), [1.5M Q&A](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lidFtb1Ga0o&t=2082s), [Email Campaign](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OpeN4O5myIg&t=1206s), [Top Questions](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QwEb78e5a8Y&t=755s), [Lead Quality](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Kk6Vdfdwmxw&t=319s))

## 10. Worked examples (business → what he suggested)

- **Personal stylist:**
  - a free lookbook to dress for your tone using the closet you already have;
  - or a style assessment for qualified leads only;
  - the easiest version is your colour palette from skin, hair and eyes.

## 11. Free offers that don't use the words "lead magnet" (110 passages, 53 videos)

He also calls these "attraction offers": something free or discounted that gets a customer in, with the money made on
what they buy next.

**The payback test.** Done right, a customer pays back what they cost to acquire on day one; done wrong, you lose money
on each one. ([Money Models launch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6_CCutkM11g&t=2044s), [Money Models launch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6_CCutkM11g&t=2075s))

**"Give away the secrets, sell the implementation."**
- What people pay for is implementation, accountability, troubleshooting and an outside view.
- Giving everything away doesn't eat sales, because people buy on value they've already received.

([1.2M Followers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WrCt0R3FBFs&t=1392s), [1.2M Followers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WrCt0R3FBFs&t=1452s), [Legit Online Course](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oTQPxPFROck&t=224s))

**Free isn't always better.** For cold traffic he'd turn a free webinar into a $99 workshop with bonuses stacked until
the price "feels absurd". By his estimate, that cuts unqualified attendees from about 90% to about 40%.
([AMA](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_ooj0V4hpgw&t=524s), [AMA](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_ooj0V4hpgw&t=585s))

**Trials need skin in the game.** He calls a bare free trial into a membership the "bad money model": a gym takes three
months to break even, and many members quit by month four. Fixes:
- a trial with a penalty: card on file, a set minimum number of sessions, a no-show fee;
- free with a deposit that's refunded or credited when they hit the goal;
- a small upfront fee that's rebated if they use the tool;
- for software, a reason to use it during the trial.

([Money Models launch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6_CCutkM11g&t=1105s), [The Offer Is King](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pxVeOkOVr2w&t=394s), [The Offer Is King](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pxVeOkOVr2w&t=425s), [Hotline](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-HbwUdqUcEs&t=4119s))
- If you take a card before a free visit, say it's "just how we've always done it". People don't argue with policy.
  ([Stranger $5M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sBJppqCeFGI&t=1641s))

**Ungated content.**
- His own free courses have no opt-in or login. In his opinion (not a test), that retains better and gets shared more.
  ([Bacon Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orMbq2LtzKE&t=4723s))
- **Tension with §5:** he gates lead magnets for ads and call-booking funnels, but leaves broad educational content open
  with an optional opt-in partway through.

**Audits and free consults work for high-ticket services.**
- Roofing and heating/cooling: "free audits or some sort of free thing" anchored on savings, on-site or virtual.
- Dental implants: a free consult plus a free scan was fine. What failed was the call that sets the appointment and the
  missing pre-visit VSL.

([Paid Ads 30 Min](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fSbqaTlWaYI&t=1578s), [dentist call](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tnDC4ZJ0oy8&t=1047s))

**"20 mistakes people make buying [your category]".** A slide deck with one customer proof under each mistake, handed
out by QR from a conference stage. The opt-in asks whether they buy this kind of thing and how often, so the opt-in
list is the lead list. ([Service-Based Businesses](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mRlSb0O5QNU&t=641s), [Service-Based Businesses](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mRlSb0O5QNU&t=672s))

**Copy:**
- Write for someone who doesn't know you, is in a hurry and reads at a third-grade level. Clear beats clever, and
  cutting beats explaining. ([13 Years Marketing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=reisEL_D7xc&t=1310s))
- Each piece sells only the next step: the ad sells the click, the headline sells the opt-in, the opt-in sells the
  show-up. ([14 Years Marketing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JDR-R--4HhM&t=643s))
- Changing only the landing-page headline raised bookings 62%, with the opt-in rate flat. Watch the step after the
  opt-in. ([13 Years Marketing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=reisEL_D7xc&t=1280s))
- Call out a narrow audience: "moms in Nevada" gets "this is for me". ([13 Years Marketing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=reisEL_D7xc&t=1500s))

**Qualifiers and friction:**
- Put the one or two data points that predict a buyer on the form, and score leads on them. For a travel business that
  was credit-card spend and vacation spend. ([Stranger $12M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MH-IMJxbUY4&t=1089s))
- **Friction is a dial.** Too many bad leads: add friction. Too few: remove it. No friction and still bad leads: the
  targeting is off. ([Paid Ads 30 Min](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fSbqaTlWaYI&t=617s))

**After the opt-in:**
- Educating someone right after they opt in costs almost nothing, so put a VSL or the scheduler on the thank-you page.
  ([Top 1%](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=noE1MQPMvAc&t=2794s), [Paid Ads 30 Min](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fSbqaTlWaYI&t=586s))
- Call everyone who opts in. "Conversions happen in conversations."
  ([Paid Ads 30 Min](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fSbqaTlWaYI&t=1609s), [34.2M Views](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eW04CvHKvMo&t=5121s))

**Don't polish the opt-in rate forever.** Taking a 30% page to 45% never 10x's a business; send more traffic instead.
Judge sources by what a customer costs, not by cost per opt-in.
([Hotline](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-HbwUdqUcEs&t=1589s), [Scale a Service Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N5MExtki_VI&t=1937s))

  ([Leads launch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k834E6OTGTM&t=1316s))
